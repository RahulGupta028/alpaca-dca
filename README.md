#Monthly Limit Buy with DAY Order Reposting

Improves upon traditional DCA by placing limit orders at a 5% discount below the previous day's close instead of buying at market price. Since allocated investment amounts often result in fractional shares that brokerages won't allow on persistent limit orders, this strategy automatically reposts DAY orders each trading day until the full tranche amount fills, maximizing the chance of getting better entry prices while maintaining fractional order capability.
